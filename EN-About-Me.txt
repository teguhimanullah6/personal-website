EN-About-Me
Teguh Imanullah, born in Jambi City on October 10, 1998, is an Indonesian activist, PhD student and youth diplomacy practitioner. He has resided in Russia for approximately 10 years, beginning with his undergraduate (S1) studies. He is currently pursuing a doctoral (S3) degree in Powder Metallurgy and Composite Materials at Peter the Great St. Petersburg Polytechnic University. In 2023, he completed his master’s (S2) degree at the same university and department, graduating summa cum laude. Teguh is actively engaged in various youth activities and public diplomacy. He devoted five years to the Indonesian Students’ Association in Russia (PERMIRA), rising from a grassroots cadre to its highest leadership position. He has received the “Best Student” award in International Cooperation from the Government of St. Petersburg (2022), the “People’s Diplomacy Leader” award from the Eurasian Peoples’ Assembly (2022), and the Ki Hadjar Dewantara Awards from the Ambassador of the Republic of Indonesia to the Russian Federation, concurrently accredited to the Republic of Belarus, Jose Antonio Morato Tavares, for his contributions in the areas of social affairs, diplomacy, and leadership (2026). Teguh has also been a speaker at more than 10 international forums, focusing on youth empowerment and education to strengthen Indonesia–Russia collaboration. He currently serves as Chair of the Representative Body of the Indonesian National Youth Committee (KNPI) for the Russian Federation, and as Advisor of Youth Affairs for the President of the Alliance of Business Structures and Entrepreneurs of Southeast Asia (B2BASEAN).
