Where it began.

From Jambi, Indonesia, to a life shaped by research, learning, and connection across cultures.

Roots in Indonesia

I am Indonesian and come from Jambi. My background continues to shape how I approach both scientific work and public engagement: with curiosity, a sense of responsibility, and an interest in how people can learn from one another.

A path across disciplines

My work brings together materials science and public diplomacy. In the laboratory, I study powder metallurgy and composite materials. Beyond it, I contribute to youth initiatives and dialogue connecting Indonesia and Russia.
